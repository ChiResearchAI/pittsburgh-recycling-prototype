What you are looking at

Why this, first

Residents are already asking this question — by telephone

In the City's own 311 record there are 11,390 "Recycling/Refuse/Blue Bin Questions", and 70.2% of them arrive by phone. The demand this application serves is not a projection. It is already being absorbed by City staff, one call at a time.

Set-out location

The rear and side exception set is first-class data

Roughly a fifth of Pittsburgh addresses are set out at the rear or side rather than the curb, and Environmental Services already maintains that in RouteSmart. Most applications show every household the same curb graphic. This one renders the instruction that applies to your address — try the Troy Hill and Beechview samples and watch the set-out line change.

The City confirmed at Questions 19 and 54 that a periodic export, not a live integration, is the intended mechanism. So the import is a repeatable validated load with a reconciliation report on every run — never a silent success.

Governance

Every answer traces to a human-authored rule

Open "Why this answer" on any result. You get the rule text, who owns it, and when it was last reviewed. Per the City's answer at Question 20, the disposal answer is always served from the Pittsburgh rule — never generated by a model, and no City data trains anything.

That is also what makes the catalogue maintainable: Environmental Services edits the rule, and every surface that quotes it changes at once.

Honest scope

What this prototype is not

It is a weekend build of one flow, to show the shape of the thing and that we can build it. The administrative console, the notification and geo-targeting engine, the analytics views and the accessibility conformance work are described in the Response to Scope and priced in the Cost Proposal — they are not in this page.

The material rules here are an illustrative sample. In delivery, ChiResearch supplies a base catalogue and Environmental Services co-develops it from there, which is the arrangement the City described at Question 55.
